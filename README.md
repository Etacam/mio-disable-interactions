# DisableInteractions

Adds options to disable many of Mio's interactions.

This mod is mostly something to help figure out logic for potential future rando features.

Things you can disable are:
* Slash being able to hit most objects:
    * Energy Crystals
    * Energy Flowers
    * Bombs
    * Spring flowers
    * Junk Piles
    * Breakable Walls
    * Splodge crystals
    * Crucible Spinners
* Hairpin being able to hit most things:
    * Energy Crystals
    * Enemies (except bosses)
    * Splodge Crystals
* Being able to be hit by bombs (and thus being able to dodge them for energy)
* Slingshot being able to hit energy shields
